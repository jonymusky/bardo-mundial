london|London on an overcast late afternoon: the Big Ben clock tower at the centre, a giant observation wheel, Tower Bridge with its two towers, a dome cathedral and a few modern glass towers. Moody teal-grey layered clouds with a pale warm cream break of light near the horizon, a few lit windows.

Sky from near-black violet at the top through purple to hot magenta and orange at the horizon, a large pale pink full moon in the upper left, cyan and pink lit windows.

Sky from deep ocean blue at the top through turquoise to warm yellow-orange at the horizon, sun low on the right third.
sydney|Sydney on a bright clear day: the white sail-shaped Opera House at the centre, the steel arch Harbour Bridge behind it, modern glass towers and a slender observation tower. Vivid blue sky fading to pale cream at the horizon, a few bright white cumulus clouds, bright high sun on the right.
